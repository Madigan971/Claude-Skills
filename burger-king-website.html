08 — The Flame Behind the Brand
1954

In Miami, 1954, two brothers lit a flame that would circle the globe. They didn't have algorithms or focus groups — they had an open broiler and a belief that real fire makes real flavour.

Seventy years later, that flame is still burning. Every Whopper® is still grilled the same way — over a real open flame, never a flat-top, never a shortcut.

We use 100% pure beef with no fillers, no artificial preservatives, and no apologies. Our packaging is moving to 100% recyclable materials by 2026, and we've committed to net-zero operations by 2050.

🔥
Flame-Grilled Always
Every patty cooked over real open fire. Not a flat-top. Not steam. Fire.
🐄
100% Real Beef
No fillers, no additives, no artificial preservatives in our beef patties.
🌍
Sustainability Commitment
100% recyclable guest packaging by 2025. Net-zero operations target: 2050.
